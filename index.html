FIELD GUIDE

Make a world.
Let them learn it.

  • Food tool: left-click in the field to drop a pile. Right-click a pile to remove it.
  • Obstacle tool: click to place a rock. Workers learn to route around it.
  • Eraser: click a food pile or rock to remove it.
  • Worker collisions: workers have solid personal space. A collision during a food pickup is an attack; both workers die.
  • Simulation: pause, resume, or clear your edits whenever you like.
  • Learning: workers update a small tabular Q-learning policy as they explore, collect food, and hit obstacles.

Spread food piles apart to reduce fights. Workers restart fresh; the learned colony brain is saved locally.
