THE ONLY RULE IS: THERE ARE NO RULES

How to play
Wonderworks.

  1. Pick ingredients. Choose one unexpected thing, one point of view, and one playful rule.
  2. Make it yours. Name your invention, write a sentence, or sketch a prototype. Do as much or as little as you want.
  3. Plant the idea. It goes in your private Idea Vault. Sparks celebrate experimenting — they are not a score of your talent.

Try a combination you wouldn't normally choose. Follow the strange bit. Change your mind whenever you like.
